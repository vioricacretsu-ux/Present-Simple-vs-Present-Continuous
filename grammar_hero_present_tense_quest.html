Grammar Cheat Sheet

Present Simple

Use for habits, routines, permanent situations, and general truths.

Form: Subject + Verb (s/es for He/She/It)

Example: She walks to school every day.

Key Time Markers (Signal Words):
usually always every day often never on Mondays
Present Continuous

Use for actions happening RIGHT NOW or temporary situations.

Form: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing

Example: She is walking to school right now.

Key Time Markers (Signal Words):
now right now at the moment Look! Listen! today

Pro Tip: State Verbs

Verbs like like, love, want, know, understand, believe are usually ONLY used in Present Simple, even if happening right now!

✓ "I want water right now." (Not: "I am wanting...")
